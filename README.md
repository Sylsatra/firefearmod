# **Fear of Fire**

### **Mobs now FEAR fire! Mobs will run away from fire, lava, and campfires. A game-changer for base defense, mob control, and creating safe zones. A must-have survival utility!**

***

## They Fear The Flames…

Ever placed a campfire and thought, "Why aren't the zombies scared of this?" **Fear of Fire** is a simple, server-side mod that adds a new, intuitive layer to mob AI: **Mobs are now terrified of sources of fire and will actively run away from them.**

No longer is fire just a source of light and a way to cook food. It is now your greatest tool for **base defense**, **mob control**, and **creating safe zones** on the fly. Turn your base into a fortress that mobs won't even dare to approach!

***

## Key Features

*   🔥 **Intelligent Fleeing AI:** Mobs will actively detect and flee from fire-based threats. They won't just stand there to burn; they will turn and run for their lives!
*   🛡️ **Ultimate Base Defense:** Create impenetrable perimeters using moats of lava, walls of fire, or strategically placed campfires. Finally, a **creeper-proof** defense that doesn't rely on cats or complex blast shields!
*   🧭 **Strategic Mob Control:** Use fire to your advantage! Herd mobs into traps, guide them away from your mineshaft, or create a temporary safe bubble in a cave with a single campfire.
*   🧟 **Fungal Infection: Spore Integration:** The highly aggressive and terrifying infected mobs from the **Fungal Infection: Spore** mod are also smart enough to fear the flames, giving you a fighting chance against the horde.
*   ⚙️ **Fully Configurable (Via Tags):** Total control for pack makers! Use data packs to easily define which mobs should fear fire and which blocks should scare them away.

***

## What Scares Them?

By default, mobs will flee from the following blocks:

*   **Fire & Soul Fire**
*   **Lava** (including flowing lava)
*   **Campfires & Soul Campfires**
*   **Magma Blocks**

You can add or remove blocks using the config.

***

## Use Cases

*   **The Survivalist:** Build a truly safe base where mobs won't even touch your walls.
*   **The Explorer:** Carry a campfire in your inventory to instantly create a temporary safe zone while you organize your inventory or plan your next move in a dark cave.
*   **The Technical Player:** Design new mob farms or sorting systems by using fire to guide mobs along specific paths.
*   **Modpack Creators:** Add a realistic and configurable survival mechanic that deepens the gameplay experience.

### FAQ

**Q: Does this work with mobs from other mods?** A: Yes! By default, it only affects all mobs from vanilla & Fungal Infection: Spore. You can customize which mobs are affected config.

**Q: Is it for Forge or Fabric?** A: This is currently for Forge only.

**Q: Will this cause server lag?** A: The mod is designed to be very lightweight. The AI checks are optimized for minimal performance impact and it should run smoothly on servers.
